# CLAUDE.md — Pathways USA

Pathways USA is a free U.S. citizenship test prep app (civics, reading, writing, speaking, N-400 guide). It was built in Base44 and exported to this repo.

**Redesign in progress (Nov 2026): Pathways + Amio.** On the `standalone` branch the app is becoming a course of small 5-step lessons with an AI tutor character, Amio (formerly "Vía"), and is moving from Base44 to Supabase (Standalone Steps 0–3). The rules below are the new rules. "Current look in code" and the Stack section describe the app as it is today; where they disagree with the rules, the rules win, and each redesign prompt updates the description it changes.

## Rules (always follow)

### Legal and content
- Pathways USA is a free education app, **not a legal service**. Nothing may tell a learner how THEY should answer an N-400 question about their own history, or whether THEY are eligible. Point legal questions to the "Talk to a lawyer first" section of the Resource Hub (`src/pages/LawyerPage.jsx`, `/lawyer-first`).
- Official civics questions and answers come from the app's USCIS question lists (`CivicsQuestion` records, versions `2008` and `2025`). AI may **explain** them but must never change, add, or invent official answers.
- **Personal N-400 questions** are only ever practiced as a made-up **practice person**, always labeled "practice person, not real" (a trial: we'll see if we like it). The practice person's answers are content, reviewed like other N-400 wording, never AI-written live. Every N-400 card keeps "If yes might be true for you, talk to a lawyer first."
- Wherever the app meets the public or the test, show "Free practice · Not affiliated with USCIS · Not legal advice". The AI officer is always labeled "AI, not a real person."
- **Course content** (lessons, sentences, explanations) is reviewed content in the repo (`content/course/`), never generated live by AI. Learners see only `"reviewed"` lessons. Read it / Write it sentences use only words from the official USCIS reading and writing vocabulary lists (`content/vocab/`, copied word for word, never retyped). Course content is 2025-only for now.
- **Current officeholders and capitals** (President, Vice President, Speaker, Chief Justice, U.S. senators, representatives and delegates, governors, D.C. mayor, state capitals) come **only** from `src/data/officeholders.json` (checked monthly against official government sites; logic in `src/lib/officeholders.js`, which is replaced, never hand-edited). The AI never supplies these names from memory or a web search. The 16 civics questions about them (`OFFICEHOLDER_QUESTIONS` in `src/lib/officeholderQuestions.js`, matched by official wording) are graded on the device with `gradeOfficeholderAnswer()`; `correct === null` counts as neither right nor wrong. Ask Vía gets `VIA_OFFICEHOLDER_RULES` + `buildViaOfficeholderContext()` and no web search. Ask Vía also gets the official 2025 civics list (`loadCivicsBank()` in `src/lib/civicsBank.js`, from `content/civics/questions-2025.json`, loaded once per page) with official numbers and 65/20 marks only once `content/civics/numbers-2025.json` covers every question; until then Vía is not asked for numbers.

### Privacy
- Never store a learner's immigration status, A-number, case details, filing date, field office, address, or N-400 answers.
- A learner's location (browser location or typed ZIP on the Resource Hub) is used on the device only, to sort places by distance: never saved, never in a URL, never sent to the AI or Base44.
- Never record or store audio. Speech becomes text in the browser; only text is sent for grading, and what the learner said is never saved; only scores.
- The learner's interview month / target date lives **only** in browser storage on their device, never in the database (wrap every `localStorage` read/write in try/catch). An address is never stored (ZIP only, used once).
- Partner programs see only the learners who picked them: the name the learner gave (or their email) and whether they confirmed them, plus counts (lessons completed, stars, checkpoints passed, and the counts listed under Partner programs). Never answers, Amio conversations, notes, pilot answers, N-400 section names or case details. In the redesign prompts "tutor" means partner program staff; there are no tutor accounts.
- **Every saved data type is covered by "Delete all my data"** (`src/lib/deleteMyData.js`; its test fails if a learner table is missing) and, where it goes into the pilot export, as counts keyed by the random `pilot_id` only.
- Pilot outcome questions ("Help us improve Pathways", AI Policy "Pilot Check-ins") are optional fixed choices with "I'd rather not say": no free text, dates, case numbers or details. Only the learner and admins may ever see them; never partner staff, never the AI.
- Learner text (typed or spoken-as-text) goes to the AI only in the features the AI Policy describes: Ask Vía, civics answer grading, interview/lesson answer scoring, and the representative lookup (zip, plus a street address only when the zip spans several districts; the AI returns only the state and district). Never with personal identifiers (email, name), never saved, and emails, phone numbers, A-numbers and Social Security numbers are stripped first where learners type freely (`redactIdentifiers()` in `src/lib/askTutor.js`). Check answers on the device first when that's enough.

### Design (Pathways + Amio)
- **Binding spec**: `docs/redesign/amio-style-guide.md` (Nov 13, 2026; the style guide word for word plus the decisions made with it), with the canvas frames in `docs/design/` (R30…R33 names). It replaces "The Road" (`docs/redesign/road-style-guide.md`) and `brand-spec.md`, both kept for history. The redesign lands one prompt at a time (Prompts 1–13) on branches from `standalone`; screens not yet redone keep the Road layout.
- **Type**: Lexend only (400/500/600/700/800): headings and buttons 700, labels 600, body 400, celebration titles 800. Sizes: screen title 20px, lesson question 22–26px, celebration title 28–32px, body 15–16px, small 12–13px, never below 12px. Other scripts fall back to Noto (Sans, Arabic, SC, Devanagari, Ethiopic), loaded only when that language is chosen. No other fonts.
- **Colors** live in ONE place with the style-guide names (`navy`, `denim`, `red`, `red-text`, `gold`, `page`, `tint`, `mid`, `line`, `chip-navy`, `muted`, `on-navy`, `correct`, `not-yet`, the journey colors and the stop-type colors). Use the names, never one-off hex. **Denim** is the main action (buttons, progress, the current step, the learner's answers; white text). **Red** is Amio, the "you are here" stop and the little flag only: never small text, never errors (`red-text` where red needs white text on it, e.g. "end call"). **Gold** is stars, rewards, badges and Speak it! checkpoints, with navy text. `correct` always comes with a check icon; `not-yet` always with words, never color alone. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
- **Layout**: a navy banner on top (title, where you are, stars, progress), the page color below, white cards with 20–24px corners. One main button per screen: denim, 56px tall, 16px corners, a 5px navy "lip" under it; second button white with a line border; chips white with a line border, fully rounded. Tap targets ≥ 44×44px. Simple outline icons with a 2px stroke; no emoji anywhere.
- **Navigation**: four bottom tabs, Today · Course · Interview · Me (white bar, active tab denim). The N-400 guide, Interview Day and the mock interview live under Interview; Ask Amio, Free help near you (Resource Hub), Settings and My program under Me. No floating tutor button. Old routes keep working (redirect when moved).
- **Amio** is the tutor: one character, always named Amio (learners no longer name the tutor; an old saved name such as "Vía" is ignored). One inline-SVG component with `mood` (happy, wave, curious, listen, kind, cheer, proud) and `size`, shapes from `docs/design/amio/Amio.tsx`. Amio says ONE short line in a white bubble with a 2px red border: never paragraphs, never a chat log. Labeled "Amio · AI tutor" where a label is needed. Amio is the only character besides labeled practice people and the AI officer.
- **The five rules**: (1) no blank text box for chatting: learners tap, speak or choose (typing is allowed for Write it, ZIP, search, and a small "Type instead" link wherever speaking is asked); (2) the plan comes first: Today shows today's lesson, not a conversation; (3) Amio is a character in a place saying one short line; (4) voice first: the mic is the biggest button on speaking screens; (5) lessons end: steps, a finish, "Done for today."
- **Language**: Amio explains in the learner's language (tagged "Said in your language"); everything the learner answers, reads or writes is in English. All text goes through the translation system, fits longer languages and works right-to-left (logical start/end spacing, no fixed-width text boxes, the journey path mirrors in RTL).
- **Rewards**: stars, badges (one per finished section or unit), a daily goal and a kind streak (one rest day a week; a missed day never punishes). Stars and the streak are calculated from saved progress and `ActivityDay`, never stored twice. Confetti uses `canvas-confetti` with `disableForReducedMotion: true`. No leaderboards, hearts or lives.
- Mobile-first at 360px wide. `npm run lint` is clean; keep it that way.

### Tutor tone
- Respectful and adult, warm and short. Amio is the one character (no other mascots); kind after a miss ("Not yet"), never shaming. No leaderboards, hearts/lives, or punishing streaks.

### How to work
- Change only what a prompt asks. Before building, list every file and component you'll change or add and any new data, and wait for an OK.
- Test with fake information only.
- Redesign work: branch from `standalone`, open the pull request into `standalone`, and don't merge it. Never merge into `main` (that updates the live Base44 app).
- If a screen needs data we don't have and the prompt didn't ask to add it, leave it out and say so.

## Stack

- **React 18 + Vite**, JavaScript (JSX), Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui (`src/components/ui/`), lucide icons, TanStack Query, react-router.
- **Base44** is the backend: `@base44/sdk` client in `src/api/base44Client.js`, plus `@base44/vite-plugin`.
  - **Data**: Base44 entities, defined in `base44/entities/*.jsonc`, used as `base44.entities.<Name>.list/filter/create/update`. Records (including the civics question bank, interview questions, reading/writing sentences) live in the Base44 database, not in repo files.
  - **Access rules**: each entity file has an `rls` block. Content (questions, sentences, lessons, vocabulary) is public to read and admin-only to change. Learner records are owner-only (`data.user_email` = the signed-in email); `ProgramMembership` is read/delete by its owner and created/changed only by the `partner` function; `Partner` is admin-only. Admins get no extra access to learner data, with two pilot exceptions: `CheckInReminder` (which pilot reminder emails were sent) is admin-only and written only by the `pilot-reminders` function, and the `pilot-report` function gives admins one summary per learner keyed by `pilot_id` (never an email or name) for the pilot dashboard. Keep stored emails lowercase so the rules match. A new entity with no `rls` is open to every user.
  - **Auth/user**: `base44.auth.me()` / `base44.auth.updateMe()` (the `User` entity holds extra fields). Everyone who signs in is a learner (no roles; there are no human tutor accounts): `Layout.jsx` makes the `PublicProfile` on the first sign-in (`ensurePublicProfile()`, `src/lib/profileService.js`) and opens `/quick-check`.
  - **Backend functions**: `base44/functions/<name>/entry.js` (shared code in `base44/shared/`), called with `base44.functions.invoke(name, data)`. `partner` (pure logic in `base44/shared/partners.js`, client helpers in `src/lib/partners.js`): `list` active programs, `join` (learner picks a program → pending `ProgramMembership`), `staff`, `members`, `setStatus` (the program's staff confirm or mark "not ours") and `summary` (totals; the only backend code that reads `ActivityDay`, to count). The app never writes `ProgramMembership` itself (a learner may delete their own). `pilot-report` (admins only, 404 for anyone else) returns the pilot dashboard's per-learner summaries (`learnerSummary()` in `base44/shared/pilotReport.js`; the program is the learner's `ProgramMembership` unless the program said "not ours"; it also makes a missing `pilot_id`). `pilot-reminders` sends the pilot check-in reminder emails (`send`, admins only, counts only) and deletes a learner's own reminder records (`forget`, for Delete my data).
  - **AI**: `base44.integrations.Core.InvokeLLM({ prompt, response_json_schema?, file_urls?, add_context_from_internet? })`. Also `SendEmail`, `UploadFile`.
- **AI tutor persona** (default name "Vía"): config and `buildTutorInstructions()` in `src/lib/tutorPersona.js`; UI in `src/components/persona/`.
- **Tutor lessons**: `TutorLesson` (`src/components/persona/`) renders a lesson = `{ id, title, steps }`; step types and rules in `src/lib/tutorLesson.js`. Practice checks are shared: `checkCivicsAnswer()` (`src/components/civics/`, AI, spoken answers) and `checkDictation()` / `matchOfficialAnswer()` (`src/lib/practiceChecks.js`, on-device, for typed answers). Feedback always shows official accepted answers from the question bank, never AI-written ones. Admin-only demo at `/tutor-demo`. Step types also include `part-intro` (skippable parts), `practice-reading`, `practice-interview`, and `mode: 'check'` (record the answer, no teaching).
- **Quick check (pretest)**: `/quick-check` (`src/pages/QuickCheck.jsx`); lesson building and level cutoffs in `src/lib/pretest.js`. Only the four levels are saved (`User.skill_levels`). `?part=<skill>` re-checks one skill and keeps the others. The first time on a device, **Getting started** comes first (`GettingStarted.jsx`, logic in `src/lib/onboarding.js`): a Start tap (lets the browser speak; shows Vía's mark), language, tutor name (suggestions in `SUGGESTED_TUTOR_NAMES`, `tutorPersona.js`), where they are in the process, which civics test (only if applied; stores the test version, never a date), interview month (only if scheduled; reuses `PlanSetup`), which partner program they study with (only when there are active `Partner`s; the programs plus "No, I'm studying on my own"; picking one asks for an optional name and, where the program allows it, "Share my progress"; saved through the `partner` function), then sound and text size. - **Study plan + Today**: pure plan logic in `src/lib/studyPlan.js`, device-only settings in `src/lib/planSettings.js` (target date, plan mode, checkmarks, visits), check-ins in `src/lib/checkIns.js`; UI `TodayCard.jsx` / `PlanSetup.jsx` in `src/components/persona/`, text in `todayStrings.js`. Today is a split screen, full width (`TodayCard.jsx`; Layout drops its padding on `/` for signed-in learners; helpers in `src/lib/todayView.js`). **Navy top**: a label "Stop {n} of 5 · {stop}" ("Start here" on the first day), "Good morning/afternoon/evening, {first name}." (sign-in name, shown only, never translated or sent to the AI; "Welcome." without one), Vía's line (no signature) with Listen, and the road (`src/components/home/TodayRoad.jsx`): five stops (Read and write, Learn, Speak, Interview day, Oath day; links in `roadmapStops.js` `href`, Oath day → Progress) with done = sky + check, current = star red + star + halo, later = hollow, the road behind done stops sky.
