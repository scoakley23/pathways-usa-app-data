# CLAUDE.md — Pathways USA

Pathways USA is a free U.S. citizenship test prep app (civics, reading, writing, speaking, N-400 guide). It was built in Base44 and exported to this repo.

**Redesign in progress (Nov 2026): Pathways + Amio.** On the `standalone` branch the app is becoming a course of small 5-step lessons with an AI tutor character, Amio (formerly "Vía"), and is moving from Base44 to Supabase (Standalone Steps 0–3). The rules below are the new rules. "Current look in code" and the Stack section describe the app as it is today; where they disagree with the rules, the rules win, and each redesign prompt updates the description it changes.

## Rules (always follow)

### Legal and content
- Pathways USA is a free education app, **not a legal service**. Nothing may tell a learner how THEY should answer an N-400 question about their own history, or whether THEY are eligible. Point legal questions to the "Talk to a lawyer first" section of the Resource Hub (`src/pages/LawyerPage.jsx`, `/lawyer-first`).
- Official civics questions and answers come from the app's USCIS question lists (`CivicsQuestion` records, versions `2008` and `2025`). AI may **explain** them but must never change, add, or invent official answers.
- **Personal N-400 questions** are taught as **understanding only**: learners learn what each question means and how the officer may ask it, and never answer it. There are no sample answers and no practice person (the "Maria" trial was dropped on Nov 18, 2026: an answer of "No" to almost everything could read as the right answer). Every N-400 card and lesson keeps "If yes might be true for you, talk to a lawyer first."
- Wherever the app meets the public or the test, show "Free practice · Not affiliated with USCIS · Not legal advice". **Product name** (`amio-v2/polish`): learners read "Amio" (browser tab and manifest "Amio · Citizenship test practice", the sign-in subtitle "from Pathways USA", "Help us improve Amio", "How Amio teaches", "We couldn't reach Amio"); the pilot questions ("Since you started Pathways…", "…without Pathways?") and partner and staff screens keep "Pathways". The AI officer is always labeled "AI, not a real person."
- **Course content** (lessons, sentences, explanations) is reviewed content in the repo (`content/course/`), never generated live by AI. Learners see only `"reviewed"` lessons. Read it / Write it sentences use only words from the official USCIS reading and writing vocabulary lists (`content/vocab/`, copied word for word, never retyped). Course content is 2025-only for now.
- **Current officeholders and capitals** (President, Vice President, Speaker, Chief Justice, U.S. senators, representatives and delegates, governors, D.C. mayor, state capitals) come **only** from `src/data/officeholders.json` (checked monthly against official government sites; logic in `src/lib/officeholders.js`, which is replaced, never hand-edited). The AI never supplies these names from memory or a web search. The 16 civics questions about them (`OFFICEHOLDER_QUESTIONS` in `src/lib/officeholderQuestions.js`, matched by official wording) are graded on the device with `gradeOfficeholderAnswer()`; `correct === null` counts as neither right nor wrong. Ask Amio gets `VIA_OFFICEHOLDER_RULES` + `buildViaOfficeholderContext()` and no web search. Ask Amio also gets the official 2025 civics list (`loadCivicsBank()` in `src/lib/civicsBank.js`, from `content/civics/questions-2025.json`, loaded once per page) with official numbers and 65/20 marks only once `content/civics/numbers-2025.json` covers every question; until then Amio is not asked for numbers.

### Privacy
- Never store a learner's immigration status, A-number, case details, filing date, field office, address, or N-400 answers (the one exception: "When's my interview?" keeps a filing month and field office on the device only, below).
- A learner's location (browser location or typed ZIP on the Resource Hub) is used on the device only, to sort places by distance: never saved, never in a URL, never sent to the AI or Base44.
- Never record or store audio. Speech becomes text in the browser; only text is sent for grading, and what the learner said is never saved; only scores.
- The learner's interview month / target date lives **only** in browser storage on their device, never in the database (wrap every `localStorage` read/write in try/catch). An address is never stored (ZIP only, used once).
- **"When's my interview?"** (frame `WhenInterview`): the learner's field office and filing month stay **on the device only** (browser storage, never the database, never in a URL, never sent to the AI). The time ranges come from a bundled file in the repo that a person checks by hand; the app never asks USCIS (or any other site) live. Every estimate says "Estimate only. Not legal advice." Built in `amio-v2/interview`: `/interview/when` (`src/components/interview/WhenInterviewPage.jsx`), logic in `src/lib/interviewEstimate.js` (`pathways_field_office`, `pathways_filed_month`; `estimateRange()`, `savedRange()`, `visibleOffices()`, `processingProblems()`), data `content/uscis/n400-processing-times.json` (filled in by hand, see `content/uscis/README.md`; `sample: true` offices only for admins and dev; `interviewEstimate.test.js` fails on a real office without `checked_on` or sensible months). The USCIS months are when the case is usually finished, not the interview date: "Most N-400s at this office are finished between {month} and {month}. Your interview usually comes before that." With no real office yet, learners see "Coming soon" and the USCIS link.
- Partner programs see only the learners who picked them: the name the learner gave (or their email) and whether they confirmed them, plus counts (lessons completed, stars, checkpoints passed, and the counts listed under Partner programs). Never answers, Amio conversations, notes, pilot answers, N-400 section names or case details. In the redesign prompts "tutor" means partner program staff; there are no tutor accounts.
- **Every saved data type is covered by "Delete all my data"** (`src/lib/deleteMyData.js`; its test fails if a learner table is missing) and, where it goes into the pilot export, as counts keyed by the random `pilot_id` only.
- Pilot outcome questions ("Help us improve Amio", AI Policy "Pilot Check-ins") are optional fixed choices with "I'd rather not say": no free text, dates, case numbers or details. Only the learner and admins may ever see them; never partner staff, never the AI.
- Learner text (typed or spoken-as-text) goes to the AI only in the features the AI Policy describes: Ask Amio, civics answer grading, interview/lesson answer scoring, and the representative lookup (the zip is looked up on the device in the Census table; a street address only when the zip spans several districts or isn't in the table, sent once to Geocodio on Supabase or the AI on Base44, which return only the state and district). Never with personal identifiers (email, name), never saved, and emails, phone numbers, A-numbers and Social Security numbers are stripped first where learners type freely (`redactIdentifiers()` in `src/lib/askTutor.js`). Check answers on the device first when that's enough.

### Design (Pathways + Amio)
- **Design source of truth (v2, Nov 18, 2026)**: `docs/design-v2/` (`README.md`, `tokens.css`, `fonts/`, `amio/Amio.tsx`, `frames/<Name>.png` + `.html`). Where the v2 frames disagree with anything older (`docs/design/`, the style guide below, the R30…R33 frames), the v2 frames win. The rules in this section that still describe the older look are replaced as each v2 prompt lands.
- **Older binding spec** (still applies where v2 says nothing): `docs/redesign/amio-style-guide.md` (Nov 13, 2026; the style guide word for word plus the decisions made with it), with the canvas frames in `docs/design/` (R30…R33 names). It replaces "The Road" (`docs/redesign/road-style-guide.md`) and `brand-spec.md`, both kept for history. The redesign lands one prompt at a time (Prompts 1–13) on branches from `standalone`; screens not yet redone keep the Road layout.
- **Type**: two fonts, both self-hosted (never Google Fonts): **Lexend** for body text, labels and buttons, and **Bricolage Grotesque 800 for headlines only** (and big numbers, per `docs/design-v2/tokens.css`). Sizes: screen title 20px, lesson question 22–26px, celebration title 28–32px, body 15–16px, small 12–13px, never below 12px. Other scripts fall back to Noto (Sans, Arabic, SC, Devanagari, Ethiopic), loaded only when that language is chosen. No other fonts.
- **Minutes**: the only place minutes appear is the plan picker: "about 5 / 10 / 15 min a day", which map to `lessons_per_day` 1 / 2 / 3. No timers or countdowns anywhere, and no minute counts on lessons, units or the quick check.
- **Nothing is locked**: learners can open any topic and any lesson. The quick check only suggests where to start; the course order is a suggestion, never a gate.
- **Colors** live in ONE place with the style-guide names (`navy`, `denim`, `amio-red` (older name `red`), `red-text`, `gold`, `sky`, `paper`, `tint`, `mid`, `line`, `line-soft`, `chip-navy`, `muted`, `on-navy`, `correct`, `not-yet`, the journey colors and the stop-type colors). Use the names, never one-off hex. **Navy** is the main action (the main button: navy with white text, a 2.5px `navy-deep` outline and lip; on a navy screen the main button is **gold** with navy text). **Denim** is the Reading colour and a secondary dark block. **Red means Amio or a celebration. Never a button, tab, border, tag, warning or wrong answer.** (`amio-v2/finishing`; also in `docs/design-v2/tokens.css`.) Amio red appears only in Amio and `AmioSays` (bubble fill `amio-tint` #FFE9E4 with an `amio-red` outline, everywhere Amio speaks), the confetti (`CONFETTI_COLORS`), a celebration headline's final punctuation (`RedStop.jsx`: Lesson done, Done for today, Unit complete), today's square in the days-this-week row once today's goal is met (`WeekSquares.jsx` on Lesson done and Done for today, `ThisWeekCard.jsx` on Progress; `goalMet()` in `todayView.js`), and the reached milestones on Me's path (an `amio-red` dot with a white ring and a small red flag); plus the Civil War map's `red-text` (the owner's choice). `src/design/red.test.js` fails on red anywhere else. Never small text. **Gold** is stars, rewards, badges and Speak it! checkpoints, with navy text. `correct` always comes with a check icon; `not-yet` always with words, never color alone. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
- **Layout** (`amio-v2/polish`): the page color (paper) with blocks and cards at **12px corners everywhere**; bottom panels and sheets 20px (top corners). **Nothing is pill-shaped except the mic** (round); `rounded-full` is for circles only (dots, icons, Amio, the mic); chips, tags and switches are 12px (`src/design/shapes.test.js` fails on a pill). **No uppercase text** anywhere a learner sees (the same test). One main button per screen: navy, 56px tall, a 5px `navy-deep` lip; second button white with a line border. Tap targets ≥ 44×44px. Simple outline icons with a 2px stroke; no emoji anywhere. **Header**: on phones there is none on first open (Welcome, the first-open steps, the quick check) or on the four tabs (`HEADERLESS` / `isHeaderless()` in `src/lib/shellRoutes.js`); other pages keep a slim navy header with Amio; desktop keeps it with the four tabs; there is no language button in it (the language is in Settings). **Footer**: one small muted line at the end of every page, once: "Free practice · Not affiliated with USCIS · Not legal advice" (translated; `FOOTER_LINES` in `Layout.jsx`); Welcome shows it on its own navy screen instead.
- **Navigation**: four bottom tabs, Today · Course · Interview · Me (white bar; the open tab navy with a bold label, Today and Me filled icons; icons from frame Today: house, books, mic, person in `icons.jsx`). The N-400 guide, Interview Day and the mock interview live under Interview; Ask Amio, Free help near you (Resource Hub), Settings and My program under Me. No floating tutor button. Old routes keep working (redirect when moved).
- **Amio** is the tutor: one character, always named Amio (learners no longer name the tutor; an old saved name such as "Vía" is ignored). One inline-SVG component with `mood` (hello, listening, thinking, speaking, cheering, proud, tryAgain, waiting; older names still work) and `size`, shapes from `docs/design-v2/amio/Amio.tsx`. Amio says ONE short line in a white bubble with a 2px red border: never paragraphs, never a chat log. Labeled "Amio · AI tutor" where a label is needed. Amio is the only character besides labeled practice people and the AI officer.
- **The five rules**: (1) no blank text box for chatting: learners tap, speak or choose (typing is allowed for Write it, ZIP, search, and a small "Type instead" link wherever speaking is asked); (2) the plan comes first: Today shows today's lesson, not a conversation; (3) Amio is a character in a place saying one short line; (4) voice first: the mic is the biggest button on speaking screens; (5) lessons end: steps, a finish, "Done for today."
- **Language**: Amio explains in the learner's language (tagged "Said in your language"); everything the learner answers, reads or writes is in English. All text goes through the translation system, fits longer languages and works right-to-left (logical start/end spacing, no fixed-width text boxes, the journey path mirrors in RTL).
- **Stars and badges are hidden from learners** in the v2 design, but the data stays: `lesson_progress.stars`, `checkpoint_results`, `program_summary()` (partner counts), the pilot CSV and Delete my data keep working unchanged. Don't remove the columns, the calculations or the counts; just don't show stars or badges on learner screens.
- **Rewards** (older rule; stars and badges now hidden, see above): stars, badges (one per finished section or unit), a daily goal and a kind streak (one rest day a week; a missed day never punishes). Stars and the streak are calculated from saved progress and `ActivityDay`, never stored twice. Confetti uses `canvas-confetti` with `disableForReducedMotion: true`. No leaderboards, hearts or lives.
- Mobile-first at 360px wide. `npm run lint` is clean; keep it that way.

### How to work
- Change only what a prompt asks. Before building, list every file and component you'll change or add and any new data, and wait for an OK.
- Test with fake information only.
- Standalone work branches from `standalone` and merges into `standalone`. Never open a pull request into `main` unless I say it's cutover day.
- Redesign work follows the same rule: branch from `standalone`, open the pull request into `standalone`, and don't merge it yourself. Merging into `main` updates the live Base44 app. About once a week, merge `main` into `standalone` and list any conflicts.
- If a screen needs data we don't have and the prompt didn't ask to add it, leave it out and say so.

The chosen language is `localStorage['pathways_language']` (`src/hooks/useAppLanguage.js`; list in `LANGUAGES` in `src/lib/languages.js`, re-exported by `useLessonTranslation.js`). **Translation pack (Step 4)**: the app's fixed text and content are translated ahead of time into `content/translations/{lang}.json` (machine, Claude Opus 5.5 in batch mode via `scripts/translations/build-pack.mjs`, run by the owner with their own key; see `scripts/translations/README.md`) and `content/translations/reviewed/{lang}.json` (human-checked, wins); `src/lib/translationPack.js` loads only the chosen language (`loadPack`, `packText`, `packTree`, `fromPack`). Order: hand-written lines → reviewed → machine → missing: English when the Supabase switch is on (`PACK_ONLY`, no live AI translation; the N-400 guide and lawyer checklist show English unless the pack has every line), live AI while it's off. Every English text to translate is listed by `src/lib/translationSources.js` (`STRING_SETS` with where each list is used, `contentTexts()`) and written to `content/translations/_sources.json` by `npm run translations:sources`; `translationSources.test.js` fails if a screen passes an unregistered list to `useTranslatedLines()` or `_sources.json` is stale. Screens' line lists live in plain modules (`shellStrings.js`, `talkStrings.js`, `gettingStartedStrings.js`, `privacyStrings.js`) so the list can be collected; the switch itself is `src/api/provider.js` (`PROVIDER`). Until the pack has a language (and with the switch off), UI strings are written in English and translated on demand with `InvokeLLM`, cached per language in localStorage (`useHomeTranslation`, `useN400Translation`, `useLawyerChecklistTranslation`). `useHomeTranslation` and `useTranslatedLines` go through `translateStrings()` (`src/lib/translateStrings.js`): screens asking for the same strings share one request, big sets go out in chunks of 15 at once and show as they arrive, and only complete translations are saved. RTL languages: `ar`, `fa`, `ur`, `he`, `prs`, `ps` (`RTL_LANGUAGES` in `useAppLanguage.js`).
- **Build/deploy**: `npm run dev`, `npm run build`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` (Vitest; tests are `src/**/*.test.js`, config in `vitest.config.js`). The live app runs on Base44: this repo is two-way synced with Base44 on `main`, so **merging to `main` updates the live app**. Work on branches and merge via pull request. Running locally needs the Base44 env vars (`VITE_BASE44_APP_ID`, `VITE_BASE44_BACKEND_URL`).
