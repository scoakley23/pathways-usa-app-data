# CLAUDE.md — Pathways USA

Pathways USA is a free U.S. citizenship test prep app (civics, reading, writing, speaking, N-400 guide). It was built in Base44 and exported to this repo.

## Rules (always follow)

### Legal and content
- Pathways USA is a free education app, **not a legal service**. Nothing may tell a learner how THEY should answer an N-400 question about their own history, or whether THEY are eligible. Point legal questions to the "Talk to a lawyer first" section of the Resource Hub (`src/pages/LawyerPage.jsx`, `/lawyer-first`).
- Official civics questions and answers come from the app's USCIS question lists (`CivicsQuestion` records, versions `2008` and `2025`). AI may **explain** them but must never change, add, or invent official answers.
- **Current officeholders and capitals** (President, Vice President, Speaker, Chief Justice, U.S. senators, representatives and delegates, governors, D.C. mayor, state capitals) come **only** from `src/data/officeholders.json` (checked monthly against official government sites; logic in `src/lib/officeholders.js`, which is replaced, never hand-edited). The AI never supplies these names from memory or a web search. The 16 civics questions about them (`OFFICEHOLDER_QUESTIONS` in `src/lib/officeholderQuestions.js`, matched by official wording) are graded on the device with `gradeOfficeholderAnswer()`; `correct === null` counts as neither right nor wrong. Ask Vía gets `VIA_OFFICEHOLDER_RULES` + `buildViaOfficeholderContext()` and no web search. Ask Vía also gets the official 2025 civics list (`loadCivicsBank()` in `src/lib/civicsBank.js`, from `content/civics/questions-2025.json`, loaded once per page) with official numbers and 65/20 marks only once `content/civics/numbers-2025.json` covers every question; until then Vía is not asked for numbers.

### Privacy
- Never store a learner's immigration status, A-number, case details, filing date, field office, address, or N-400 answers.
- A learner's location (browser location or typed ZIP on the Resource Hub) is used on the device only, to sort places by distance: never saved, never in a URL, never sent to the AI or Base44.
- No audio or speech transcripts are stored; only scores.
- The learner's target date lives **only** in browser storage on their device (wrap every `localStorage` read/write in try/catch).
- Partner programs see only the learners who picked them: the name the learner gave (or their email) and whether they confirmed them. Never answers, chats, notes, pilot answers or case details.
- Pilot outcome questions ("Help us improve Pathways", AI Policy "Pilot Check-ins") are optional fixed choices with "I'd rather not say": no free text, dates, case numbers or details. Only the learner and admins may ever see them; never partner staff, never the AI.
- Learner text (typed or spoken-as-text) goes to the AI only in the features the AI Policy describes: Ask Vía, civics answer grading, interview/lesson answer scoring, and the representative lookup (zip, plus a street address only when the zip spans several districts; the AI returns only the state and district). Never with personal identifiers (email, name), never saved, and emails, phone numbers, A-numbers and Social Security numbers are stripped first where learners type freely (`redactIdentifiers()` in `src/lib/askTutor.js`). Check answers on the device first when that's enough.

- **Contrast** (≥ 4.5:1 for all text): `star` is for fills with white text (≈ 4.7:1) and decor, never small text on cream (≈ 4.2:1); white on navy ≈ 17:1; `not-correct` on cream ≈ 5.4:1; `sky` never takes white text. Hover shades are tokens too (`navy-deep`, `star-deep`, `sand-hover`).
- **Right-to-left and translation**: text must still fit when translated and work right-to-left: logical start/end spacing (`ms-`, `pe-`, `start-`), no fixed-width text boxes, back arrows flip (`rtl:rotate-180`).
- Mobile-first at 360px wide. Buttons at least 44px tall. Body text at least 16px.

### Tutor tone
- Respectful and adult. No cartoon mascots, leaderboards, hearts/lives, or punishing streaks.

### How to work
- Change only what a prompt asks. Show a plan before changing code.
- Test with fake information only.

## Stack

A lesson's questions are its topic's `CivicsQuestion`s matched to the lesson by shared words (`src/lib/lessonQuestions.js`, no extra data; each question goes to one lesson). Official questions/answers are never sent for translation. Saves only `LessonProgress` (completed, scores). `/learn` and `/review` pass `posters: true` to `TutorLesson`, so their civics questions show on posters too.
- **Practice**: the Practice tab of Study (`/study?tab=practice`, `src/pages/Practice.jsx`) hub = four navy tiles, in order Reading · Writing · Civics · Speaking ("The officer's questions"), each with a white Start that starts right away; vocabulary games (word dictation, flashcards, matching) as pills under them; "Mixed practice" → the Interview Day run-through; civics test version, difficulty and voice live in a settings sheet ("{version} test" button). A run is full screen: it lives in `?mode=` (reading, dictation, civics, interview, vocabulary) next to `tab=practice`, which hides the header, tab bar and footer (`practiceRun` in `Layout.jsx`); `RunShell` gives a close button, and Back closes it. A run ends on a summary poster (right · almost · missed, Practice again). Every item renders as a `Poster` (`index`/`total` props → color via `posterTone`, "CIVICS 2025 · 2 OF 6", `PosterProgress`: a thin white line, one segment per card; results are counted with `cardOutcome()` in `src/lib/practiceResults.js` (dictation passes a word score so a near miss is "almost") and shown only on the summary poster): `CivicsTestCard`, `ReadingPracticeCard`, `DictationCard` (`src/components/game/`; also the vocabulary Word dictation), `FlashcardGame` (word on the poster, "Show the definition" flips it to the definition, then "I knew it" / "I missed it"), and the current question in `InterviewPractice`. Matching (`MatchingGame`) is white index cards on the sand desk instead, with a navy MATCHED stamp per pair and "n of m matched"; it saves nothing and ends on the summary poster. Feedback stays on the poster ("Answer: …", official answers only); reading and writing keep the word-by-word check (`wordDiff`, `checkDictation`). Listen and write: the learner types on `RuledPaper` under the poster, and after the check the paper shows what they wrote and the sentence word by word with missed words marked. "Explain it simply" opens `ExplainSheet`, a ruled-paper sheet (reviewed explanation; the civics card's existing on-demand AI explanation only as fallback). Interview personal/morals questions (`isUnderstandingSection`) are never answered: the learner says what the officer is asking and `evaluateUnderstanding()` (`evaluateInterviewAnswer.js`) checks only understanding, with "Talk to a lawyer first" on the poster. No practice people. Poster buttons: `posterSolid` / `posterOutline`, plus `posterTool` (Listen, Translate, Play again as white outline pills), `posterLink` ("Type it instead") and `posterBigButton` (the one big white mic or play button) in `ui.jsx`; every practice card uses them. Modes and `?mode=` params, `testMode` (Interview Day) and the `InterviewSession` rules are unchanged.
- **N-400 coaching**: `/n400-coach` (`src/pages/N400Coach.jsx`, `src/lib/n400Lesson.js`, steps in `src/components/persona/n400Steps.jsx`); the practice round is understanding only (`n400-understand` steps: the officer's phrasing, the learner says what it asks, `evaluateUnderstanding()` with the plain meaning). No practice people anywhere. Saves only section completion.
- **N-400 folder**: `/n400-guide` ("The N-400, in plain words"; a Guide | See the form switch) opens in three steps on ruled paper, so it is never a wall of text at first: the folder **cover** (`N400Cover.jsx`: one short paragraph, one chip per section, "n of 7 sections read", "We never save your answers", Vía's "how it applies to you → a lawyer" linking `/lawyer-first`, "Open the folder" and "Practice with {tutor}" → `/n400-coach`) → the **section list** (`?folder=open`, `N400SectionList.jsx`: a row per section with a navy item-count block, ✓ when read) → one **section** (`?section=…`). Sections share one navy look (`TABS` / `SECTION_SOLID` in `N400Folder.jsx`). Both `/n400-guide` sections and `/n400-coach` use the sand `N400Folder` (`src/components/n400/N400Folder.jsx`): one navy `FolderTab` per section (Part 8, then the Part 9 groups), the open section's title and part, and "n of 7 sections read" (`N400SectionProgress`). The guide keeps the open section in `?section=`; coach tabs open that section's lesson. Each question card (`N400QuestionCard.jsx`) shows the form wording in `FormQuote` ("ON THE FORM · WORD FOR WORD", "— Form N-400, item {label}"), then "Explain it simply" revealing the plain meaning, words to know and officer phrasings from `n400Content.js` verbatim, Say it back, "Talk to a lawyer first" and "We never save your answers to the N-400." **See the form** (`?view=form`, `N400BlankForm.jsx`) shows the guide's parts as blank paper-form pages (one button per section, the form's words verbatim from `n400Content.js`, empty Yes/No boxes or an empty trips table, "What it asks" opens that section in the guide); nothing is filled in, picked or saved. There are no example people or example answers anywhere.
- **Interview Day**: `/interview-day` (`src/pages/InterviewDay.jsx`, `src/components/interview-day/`, rules in `src/lib/interviewDay.js` incl. `CIVICS_TEST_RULES`): a "what to expect" guide plus a full rehearsal (oath → Mock Interview questions, with the application questions understanding-only → reading → writing → civics → results). Reuses `InterviewPractice` (`rehearsal` prop: no `InterviewSession` save, no report) and the reading, dictation and civics cards (`testMode` prop: one try, no reveal/translate). **Saves nothing**: no entity writes, no browser storage (the daily reminder is not mounted on this page).
- **Ask Vía + Talk to a person**: `src/components/ai/StudyBuddy.jsx` (a `Sheet`: full screen on phones, side drawer on desktop; header = Vía's mark + "{name} · AI tutor"; Vía's replies in Inter), `TalkToPerson.jsx`, logic in `src/lib/askTutor.js`. Support email and reply time live in `src/config/support.js` (form disabled until both are set).
- **Privacy audit**: `docs/privacy-audit.md`.
- **Resource Hub**: `/resources` (also `/lawyer-first`, which scrolls to the lawyer section), page `src/pages/LawyerPage.jsx`. "Find help near you" (`src/components/resources/ResourceFinder.jsx`; map `ResourceMap.jsx` is lazy-loaded Leaflet with OpenFreeMap "Positron" vector tiles via MapLibre (free, no key; worker bundled with `?worker&url`), falling back to paler USGS National Map tiles without WebGL; OSM's volunteer tile servers block apps and CARTO needs a key. Pins are colored by category (`PIN_COLORS`) and grouped with `leaflet.markercluster` (needs `leafletGlobal.js`); the map shows every place in range, the list 30 at a time; "Search this area" re-centers on the map; full-screen mode with a swipe-up list) lists `CommunityResource` places (categories: legal, esl, citizenship_class, uscis, library, other) near the learner. Logic and import cleaning in `src/lib/resourceHub.js`; ZIP coordinates from the bundled Census table `src/data/zipCentroids.json`. A place has one main `category` plus a `services` list (filters match any; `placeServices()`), optional `hours`, `logo_url` (shown only in the pin summary, `PlaceSummary.jsx`) and an `external_id` (`nld:…`, `doj:…`, `uscis:…`). Places are human-checked listings only (never AI-found), managed in Admin → Places (add/edit/hide, CSV/JSON import that adds new places and updates matching ones via `planImport()`; format in `content/resources/README.md`). Places that aren't relevant are hidden, not deleted, with the reason in `relevance_note`. Signed-in learners can **Save** a place (bookmark button in the pin summary and list cards, `SaveButton` in `PlaceSummary.jsx`; never a heart): owner-only `SavedPlace` records (email + resource id only) via `useSavedPlaces()`, shown as "Your saved places" at the top of the results (`savedResources()`). Logos come from NLD or the place's own website (`scripts/resources/find_logos.py`, no AI, no seals).
- **Pages** are auto-registered from `src/pages/` (`src/pages.config.js` is auto-generated; only `mainPage` is editable). `src/Layout.jsx` wraps all pages. Feature components live in `src/components/<area>/`.
- **Speech**: browser Web Speech API (`SpeechRecognition`, `lang = 'en-US'`) produces a transcript; scoring is word matching (`src/components/utils/wordDiff.js`) plus an `InvokeLLM` evaluation. Text-to-speech: `src/components/utils/textToSpeech.jsx` (`speakText(text, lang)`).
- **Languages/i18n**: no i18n library. The chosen language is `localStorage['pathways_language']` (`src/hooks/useAppLanguage.js`; list in `LANGUAGES` in `src/hooks/useLessonTranslation.js`). UI strings are written in English and translated on demand with `InvokeLLM`, cached per language in localStorage (`useHomeTranslation`, `useN400Translation`, `useLawyerChecklistTranslation`). RTL languages: `ar`, `fa`, `ur`, `he`, `prs`, `ps` (`RTL_LANGUAGES` in `useAppLanguage.js`).
- **Build/deploy**: `npm run dev`, `npm run build`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` (Vitest; tests are `src/**/*.test.js`, config in `vitest.config.js`). The live app runs on Base44: this repo is two-way synced with Base44 on `main`, so **merging to `main` updates the live app**. Work on branches and merge via pull request. Running locally needs the Base44 env vars (`VITE_BASE44_APP_ID`, `VITE_BASE44_BACKEND_URL`).
