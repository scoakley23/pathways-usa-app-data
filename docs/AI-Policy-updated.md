## Purpose of This Policy
This policy establishes guidelines for the responsible use of artificial intelligence at Pathways USA. An AI policy is essential to ensure that technology serves learners ethically, protects their privacy, and aligns with our mission to support adult learners preparing for the U.S. Citizenship Test.

## AI Use
AI tools are used to provide personalized study guidance and track learner progress through an application built on base44, which utilizes third-party AI platforms. These tools are designed to enhance the learning experience by adapting to individual needs and monitoring advancement toward citizenship test readiness. Annual assessments will be conducted to evaluate the impact of AI tools on learner outcomes and ensure they effectively support our educational mission.

Pathways USA is an education app, not a legal service. The AI tutor never tells a learner how they should answer a question about their own history on Form N-400, or whether they are eligible, and it points legal questions to the app's "Talk to a lawyer first" page.

## Interactions with Learners
AI tools interact directly with learners through the application. Each learner has an AI tutor (named "Vía" by default; the learner can rename it and choose its tone and how much English it uses; it is always shown as the Vía mark, never a face or photo). The tutor explains lessons, gives feedback on practice answers, builds a daily study plan, and answers study questions. Learners may contact a person at any time (see "Talk to a Person"). Feedback is gathered primarily through monitoring usage patterns and learner engagement data, supplemented by direct communication channels for questions or concerns.

## Data Collection Practices
Personal information including names, email addresses, and study progress data is collected to personalize the learning experience and track advancement. All data collection practices will be clearly communicated to learners through the privacy policy and terms of service provided at the start of their engagement with the application. Learners retain full ownership of their data and may request deletion at any time, and they will be informed about their rights regarding their information.

Pathways USA never asks for or stores a learner's immigration status, A-number, case details, filing date, USCIS field office, home address, or answers to Form N-400.

## Speech Input and Voice Data
Some practice activities let learners answer out loud (civics questions, reading sentences, the mock interview, tutor lessons, the quick check, and N-400 coaching). Speech is converted to text by the learner's own web browser using its built-in speech recognition (for example, Google's speech service in Chrome or Apple's in Safari). Pathways USA never receives, records, or stores audio. The browser's speech provider processes the audio under its own terms and privacy policy, which Pathways USA does not control.

The resulting transcript is used only to score the answer. It is held temporarily in the page's memory and discarded when the learner moves on; it is never saved.

What is saved is a progress record for each practice item: best and latest score (0–100), whether the answer was correct, number of attempts, when it was last practiced, and spaced-repetition values used to decide when to review it again. This record is linked to the learner's email address, never to their voice. It powers the learner's progress charts and daily plan.

## Civics Practice Grading
In the civics quiz and tutor lessons, an answer that clearly matches one of the official accepted answers is checked on the learner's device and is not sent anywhere. Other answers are graded by the AI service built into the base44 platform: the app sends the question, the learner's answer as text (with any email address or phone number removed), and the expected answer (or the learner's own saved answer from "find your representative"). The AI judges whether the meaning is correct. No name, email address, or other account information is sent. The answer itself is not saved; only the progress record described above is. In the civics lessons ("Go over them"), typed answers are checked only on the learner's device and spoken answers are graded as described here; "Explain it simply" shows the human-reviewed explanation, never one written by the AI while the learner studies, and lesson content and flashcards are translated as before but the official questions and answers are not sent for translation.

Official civics questions and accepted answers come only from the official USCIS lists in the app. The AI may explain them but never changes or adds official answers.

Some civics answers change over time (for example, the current President or Speaker of the House). For these, the AI service checks current public information on the web to grade the answer; the question and the learner's answer are part of that web check, but no account information is. Answers that depend on where the learner lives (their U.S. senators, U.S. representative, governor, and state capital) are checked against the learner's saved "find your representative" result, or by the same web check if they have not used it. Because answers are sent to an outside AI service, learners should answer only the civics question and not include personal information; the app shows this reminder under the answer box.

## Lesson Explanations
Lessons explain each civics question in the learner's language before practice. These explanations are written in advance, not generated while the learner studies. Pathways USA uses AI to draft them; the AI writes only the explanation and key words, never the official question or answers, which are copied from the official list. A person reviews every explanation before learners can see it, and an automated check blocks any explanation whose answers differ from the official list. Until an explanation has been reviewed, learners see "Explanation coming soon."

## Quick Check, Study Plan, and Target Date
**Quick check:** a short check of civics, reading, writing, and speaking. Answers are scored as described above and not saved. Only one level per skill ("Strong", "Getting there", or "Start here") and the date are saved to the learner's account, to build the study plan.

**Tutor settings:** the tutor's name, tone, and English mix are saved to the learner's account. The tutor is always shown as the Vía mark, so there is no tutor picture to choose. Learners could once use their own photo as the tutor picture, kept only in that device's browser storage; the app now deletes any such photo from the device when it loads ("Delete my data" also clears it). An old picture choice may remain in the account's tutor settings but is no longer shown or used.

**Target date and study plan:** the learner's interview or target date is saved **only on their own device** (browser storage), never to Pathways USA's database or the AI. The daily plan is calculated on the device from the learner's saved levels and progress. Daily checkmarks and visit dates are also kept only on the device. If the learner uses a new device, the app asks for the date again.

**Getting started:** before the first quick check, the app asks which language to use, what to call the tutor, and, in broad terms, where the learner is in the citizenship process (getting ready to apply, applied, interview scheduled, or just learning). If they have applied, it asks only whether Form N-400 was sent before or after October 20, 2025, to choose the 2008 or 2025 civics test. These answers are kept **only on the learner's device** and are not sent to the AI; only the tutor's name is saved to the account, as part of tutor settings. The app never asks for a filing date, case number, or field office.

**Reading aloud:** in onboarding, the quick check, and lessons, the tutor's messages are read aloud using the device's built-in voices. Nothing is recorded and no audio is sent anywhere. Learners can turn this off; the setting is saved on the device.

**Check-ins:** the app may suggest a shorter plan after missed days, more practice for a skill that has been hard, a short re-check every two weeks, or more English when scores are high. These are shown in the app only (no email or text messages) and are based on progress the app already saves.

## AI Mock Interviews
The mock interview feature lets learners practice with an AI-simulated USCIS officer. Learners answer out loud. As with other speech practice, the learner's browser converts speech to text using its built-in speech recognition service (see Speech Input and Voice Data above). Pathways USA never receives or stores audio.

In the Personal Information and Morals & Ethics sections, learners **do not answer** the questions. They say in their own words, in English, what the officer is asking, and the AI checks only whether they understood the question. It is told never to comment on facts about the learner's life, never to say how they should answer, and never to say anything about eligibility; if a learner answers instead, the app reminds them to explain the question. Emails and phone numbers are removed first. N-400 coaching uses the same understanding check, with the N-400 Guide's plain meaning of the question.

To score each answer, the app sends the text transcript, together with the interview question and the expected answer (in the understanding sections: the question and, in N-400 coaching, its plain meaning), to the AI service built into the base44 platform. The AI scores the answer for content, grammar, and fluency. The specific AI provider behind this service is not yet fully known (see Third-Party AI Tools), and that provider's own data practices apply to the text it receives.

**What is saved:** for each completed interview, the overall score, whether it passed, and each question's section and scores, linked to the learner's email address, so the learner can see their interview history. **Transcripts, the AI's written feedback, and suggested answers are not saved**; they exist only in the page's memory for the end-of-interview report.

**Please practice with sample answers:** because answers are sent to an outside AI service, learners should not give their real A-number, home address, case details, or other personal information during a mock interview.

## Interview Day (Full Rehearsal)
Interview Day (`/interview-day`) runs a practice version of the whole interview: the promise to tell the truth, small talk and an N-400 review where the learner says what each question is asking (never answers it), then reading, writing, and civics. It uses the same AI checks as the mock interview, reading practice, and civics practice, and sends the same things: the question, the expected answer, and what the learner said or typed (no name, email, or account information). **Nothing from a rehearsal is saved**: no scores, no progress, no records, and nothing in the browser's storage. Leaving or refreshing the page erases it. The same advice applies: don't share real personal information.

## Ask Vía (AI Tutor Questions)
"Ask Vía" (shown with the learner's chosen tutor name; formerly Study Buddy) answers learners' study questions in their chosen language, with the English version below. Answers are generated by the AI service built into the base44 platform, with no web search. The specific AI provider behind this service is not yet fully known; Pathways USA is reviewing base44's documentation and terms to identify it and its data practices.

**What is sent:** only the learner's question and the tutor's instructions (name, tone, and language). Email addresses and phone numbers in the question are removed first. No account information is sent.

**Questions about the learner's own case:** questions about the learner's own immigration case, history, or eligibility are declined with a kind message and a link to "Talk to a lawyer first". Many are recognized on the device and never sent to the AI. **Declined questions are not saved.**

**What is saved:** other questions and answers are stored in the learner's study history, along with the learner's email address, chosen language, and the time of the exchange, so learners can see past conversations. Only the learner can see this history. **It is not emailed or shown to anyone else.**

**Please don't share personal details:** Ask Vía is for studying. Learners should not enter their immigration status, A-number, case details, home address, or other personal information; the app shows this reminder above the chat box.

## Talk to a Person
Learners can reach a person from the Ask Vía panel with a short form. The message and the learner's account email address (so we can reply) are sent by email to pathwaysusacitizenship@gmail.com. The message is not stored in the app; it is kept in that mailbox. We reply within 3–5 business days. The form reminds learners not to share immigration status, A-numbers, case details, or addresses, and that Pathways USA cannot give legal advice.

## N-400 Guide and Coaching
The N-400 Guide explains each question on Form N-400 in plain language. N-400 coaching walks through one section at a time: what the questions mean, saying them back, and a short practice round where the officer asks a few questions and the learner says what each one is asking, without answering it. The app never collects, fills in, or stores a learner's answers to Form N-400. Only which sections the learner has completed is saved. Each section ends with a reminder to talk to a lawyer before filing if the learner is not sure an answer is "no".

## Representative Lookup and Addresses
The "find your representative" civics feature helps learners learn the names of their U.S. senators, U.S. representative and governor, and their state capital, which can be asked on the citizenship test. It starts with a zip code. If that zip code spans more than one congressional district, the app asks for a street address to find the exact district. The zip code, and the street address if one is needed, are sent to the AI service built into the base44 platform to find the officials (see Third-Party AI Tools); that provider's own data practices apply to what it receives.

The street address is used only for that lookup, in the moment, and is then cleared; the app says so next to the address box. Pathways USA never saves it to the user's profile or anywhere else. The only results the app keeps are those officials' names, the congressional district and the state capital, saved on the user's profile so civics practice can check those answers. They are erased with the rest of the profile when the user chooses "Delete my data."

Pathways USA does not keep home addresses because, for people going through the immigration process, a stored address linked to their account could put them or their households at risk.

## Resource Hub (Help Near You)
The Resource Hub shows nearby free legal help, English and citizenship classes, USCIS offices, and similar places on a map and in a list. The learner can share their location through the browser (the browser asks permission first) or type a ZIP code. The location is used only on the learner's device, in the moment, to sort places by distance: it is not saved to the account, to the device, or anywhere else, and it is not sent to the AI service or to base44. ZIP codes are turned into map coordinates using a table built into the app, so a typed ZIP code is not sent anywhere either.

The map pictures come from OpenFreeMap (a free public map service using OpenStreetMap data), or, on devices that can't draw that map, from the U.S. Geological Survey's National Map; like any web map, the service receives requests for the area being shown. "Search this area" uses the center of the map on the device only, like a typed ZIP code. "Directions" opens Google Maps with only the place's public address; the maps app asks for the starting point itself. When a learner opens a place's summary on the map, that place's logo (if it has one) loads from the National Literacy Directory's website or the place's own website; no referrer or learner information is sent with it.

Signed-in learners can tap "Save" on a place to keep it in "Your saved places" and find it again later, on any device. Only the place's id is saved, linked to the learner's email address; their location, ZIP code and searches are still never saved. Only the learner can see their saved places (not partners or the Pathways team), they can remove one at any time, and "Delete my data" removes them all. Saved places are not sent to the AI service.

The list of places is written and checked by the Pathways team from public sources (such as the USCIS office list, the U.S. Department of Justice list of recognized organizations, and the National Literacy Directory's public class listings). The AI does not find, write, or recommend places. Pathways USA does not run or endorse these places, and the page reminds learners that only lawyers and DOJ-accredited representatives can give immigration legal advice.

## Partner Programs
Some learners study with a class or program that works with Pathways USA (a "partner program"). In Getting started, and later on their Profile, a learner may say which partner program they study with, or that they study on their own. This is optional. A partner's link can pick the program in advance; the program's code waits in the browser's storage until the learner answers.

**What is saved:** which program the learner picked, whether the program has confirmed them, an optional name the learner gives so the program knows it's them (a name only; anything that looks like an email address or a number is dropped), and, only where the program allows it, whether the learner chose to share their own progress. This record is linked to the learner's email address.

**What the program sees:** the program's staff see a list of the learners who picked their program, showing the name the learner gave, or their email address if they gave none. Staff confirm each learner or mark them "not ours". They never see learners' answers, Ask Vía conversations, notes, pilot check-in answers, or anything about their case. Programs only ever see learners who picked them.

**Changing or leaving:** learners can change or leave their program on their Profile page at any time, and "Delete all my data" removes the record. None of this is sent to the AI service.

## Pilot Check-ins and Outcome Questions
Pathways USA asks learners a few optional questions to learn whether the app helps. At sign-up, a learner may share their state, where they are in the citizenship process (not filed yet, filed, or interview scheduled), and how ready they feel. At 3, 6 and 12 months, the app may ask whether they have filed, had their interview, passed the tests, taken the oath and registered to vote. Every question is optional and has an "I'd rather not say" choice. The app never asks for dates, case numbers, A-numbers, addresses or immigration status, and has no free-text boxes for these questions.

**Who can see the answers:** only Pathways USA administrators. Partner staff cannot see any learner's answers.

**Reminder emails:** only if the learner turns them on. The emails do not mention citizenship, immigration or the interview. Learners can turn them off on their Profile page.

**Activity dates:** the app records which days a learner opened it (the date only, not what they did) to measure how often people practice.

**Exports and reports:** data used for analysis uses a random ID, never names or email addresses. Pathways USA may share aggregate results (for example, "18 of 30 learners finished a mock interview") with partners, funders and government agencies. It does not sell or license individual learner data.

**Deletion:** "Delete all my data" erases check-in answers, activity dates and these profile fields.

None of these answers are sent to the AI service.

## Translations of App Text
Fixed app text (for example, menu labels and the tutor's standard messages) may be translated into the learner's language by the AI service and saved on the learner's device so it is translated only once. English and Spanish versions of key messages are written by people. No learner text is included in these translations.

## Ethical Risks and Concerns
Potential risks include bias in study recommendations that could disadvantage certain learner groups and privacy concerns related to the handling of personal information. Measures will be implemented to detect and prevent bias, with annual audits conducted to assess fairness in AI outputs.

## Accountability for AI Decisions
The founder of Pathways USA will oversee all AI-related decisions and ensure adherence to this policy. This includes monitoring AI tool performance, reviewing AI-drafted lesson explanations before they are published, addressing ethical concerns, and maintaining alignment with the organization's mission to support adult learners.

## Third-Party AI Tools
The application utilizes base44, which incorporates third-party AI platforms for development and functionality. While the specific AI tools embedded within base44 are not fully known, efforts will be made to review the terms of service and data practices of third-party providers (including whether they retain or train on data they receive) to ensure they align with our ethical standards and learner protection commitments. Speech recognition is provided by the learner's own browser, as described above.

## Staff Training and Guidance
As a sole-staff organization, the founder will manage all AI tools and maintain knowledge of AI ethics, limitations, and best practices. Written guidelines will be developed as the organization grows to ensure consistent and responsible AI use.

## Bias Audits and Fairness
Annual bias audits will be conducted to identify and address any disparities in how AI tools serve different learner populations. These audits will examine study recommendations, progress tracking, and engagement patterns to ensure equitable access and outcomes for all learners.

## Data Ownership and Learner Rights
Learners retain full ownership of their data and will be clearly informed about their rights, including the ability to request data deletion at any time. Deletion applies to data held by Pathways USA. Messages a learner sent through the "Talk to a person" form are kept in the Pathways USA mailbox and can be deleted on request.

**How deletion works:** the app's "Delete my data" option erases everything tied to the person's email address: Ask Vía history, notes, practice progress, lesson progress, mock interview history, N-400 Guide progress, saved places, pilot check-in answers, activity dates, partner program choice, notifications, and profile. It also resets the account's tutor settings, quick check levels, points, badges, display name, state and reminder setting, and clears the app's data on that device (including the target date and study plan). The sign-in account itself is deleted on request by emailing pathwaysusacitizenship@gmail.com. Transparent communication about data retention policies and timelines will be provided through the privacy policy and terms of service.

## Legal and Regulatory Compliance
Pathways USA is in the process of determining which data protection laws apply to its operations and how to ensure full compliance. This includes reviewing requirements under COPPA, GDPR, and other relevant regulations to protect learner information and maintain legal standards.

## Public Transparency and Reporting
Information about AI use will be published on the Pathways USA website through the AI policy, privacy policy, and terms of service, all of which will be provided to learners at the start of their engagement. Annual impact assessments will inform updates to these documents and ensure ongoing transparency about how AI tools are used and their effects on learners.

## Monitoring and Continuous Improvement
AI tools will be monitored continuously through learner engagement data and feedback received via direct communication channels. Annual impact assessments will evaluate whether AI tools are meeting educational goals and serving learners effectively, with adjustments made as needed to improve outcomes and address any identified concerns.
